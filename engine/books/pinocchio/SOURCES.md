## Production witness

- Carlo Collodi, *Le avventure di Pinocchio: storia di un burattino*, sixteenth edition,
  Florence: R. Bemporad & Figlio, 1899.
- Holding/digitization: Harvard University volume digitized by Google and hosted by Internet
  Archive as [`leavventuredipi00collgoog`](https://archive.org/details/leavventuredipi00collgoog).
- Acquired derivatives: image-container PDF, DjVu OCR text, positional DjVu XML, scandata XML,
  and an Internet Archive metadata snapshot.
- Internet Archive reports 266 scan pages and marks the item `NOT_IN_COPYRIGHT`.

The 1899 edition is also before the current U.S. public-domain cutoff: the
[U.S. Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/what-is-copyright/) states that works published
before January 1, 1931 are in the public domain.

The edition contains thirty-six Roman-numeral chapters, illustrations, captions, Google wrapper
matter, an index, and publisher catalog material. The structural values in `manifest.json` are
provisional bibliographic expectations; they have not yet been tested against the scan.
